Hello, we're NB

We photograph weddings the way you'll want to remember them: honestly, warmly, and with room for every generation in the frame. Most of what we do is quiet observation, waiting for the laugh between rituals and the look a parent gives when no one is watching.

We know the rhythm of multi-day celebrations, from the haldi and mehndi to the ceremony and the reception, so you can stay in the moment while we take care of the story.

  • Documentary first. Real moments, lightly guided portraits.
  • Every ritual, every generation. The details your family will ask about.
  • Made to be held. Handcrafted leather albums, designed by us.
